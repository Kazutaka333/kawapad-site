How to Make Your Own Delta Skin on iPhone

No PC, no JSON, no image editor — Kawapad: Delta Skin Editor

Can you make a Delta skin without a computer?

Yes. Kawapad is a free iOS app that lets you design custom controller skins for the Delta emulator entirely on your iPhone. Until now, making a Delta skin meant a desktop workflow: drawing artwork in an image editor and hand-writing an info.json file with button coordinates. Kawapad replaces that with drag-and-drop editing on a fullscreen canvas, at the real size of your phone.

Status: Kawapad is coming soon to the App Store. This guide describes the v1.0 release, which supports Game Boy Advance (GBA) skins.

What you need

  • An iPhone with the Delta emulator installed
  • Kawapad (free) — no account, no sign-up
  • Nothing else: no computer, no JSON editor, no drawing app

Step by step: make a skin in Kawapad

  1. Pick the GBA template. Kawapad starts from Delta's own layout geometry, in portrait and landscape.
  2. Drag the buttons where your thumbs want them. Buttons snap to a grid on the fullscreen canvas. Resize them, and the touch area always matches the artwork — in Kawapad the button art and its touch frame are one thing, so they can never drift apart.
  3. Choose the look. Apply the standard theme, or use your own photo as the background.
  4. Add action buttons (optional). Kawapad can place fast-forward, toggle fast-forward, quick save and quick load buttons — buttons most downloaded skins don't offer.
  5. Export. Tap Build/Share. Kawapad produces a standard .deltaskin file containing both portrait and landscape.

How do I import the skin into Delta?

  1. In Kawapad, tap Build/Share.
  2. In the iOS share sheet, tap Delta — the skin is imported into Delta right away.
  3. In Delta, open Settings > Controller Skins and select your skin for GBA.

If Delta doesn't appear in the share sheet, save the file to Files instead, then import it from the + button in Delta's Controller Skins settings.

Questions

Is Kawapad free?

Yes. At launch, everything in Kawapad is free — the full editor, all layouts, unlimited projects and exports, photo backgrounds, action buttons and the standard theme — with no ads. Premium themes are planned as optional extras in a later update; nothing that is free will become paid.

Which systems does Kawapad support?

Game Boy Advance (GBA) at launch. Templates for more systems are planned for later updates.

Can I edit an existing .deltaskin file in Kawapad?

No. A finished .deltaskin is one flat artwork image with the buttons baked in, so its buttons cannot be moved without breaking the artwork. Kawapad builds new skins from a template and a theme instead.

Does Kawapad include games or ROMs?

No. Kawapad only creates controller skin files. It contains no emulator and no games.

Is Kawapad affiliated with Delta?

No. Kawapad is an independent app. It makes skin files in the standard .deltaskin format for use with the Delta emulator.

Why is there a small "Kawapad" mark on exported skins?

Exports carry a small credit mark, placed automatically where it does not overlap buttons. It is how people who see your skin can find the app that made it.
