Kawapad — Privacy Policy

Kawapad collects no personal data. There is no account, no backend server, no analytics, and no ads.

Photos you import as backgrounds are picked with the system photo picker and stay on your device; they are only used to build the skin you export.

Purchases (when available) are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting support by email shares only what you write in that email.
