What this app does

Kawapad lets you design a custom controller skin for the Delta emulator (GBA to start), place the buttons on a fullscreen canvas at real size, pick a theme, and export a .deltaskin file to use in Delta. Kawapad is not affiliated with Delta, and does not claim to be official.

FAQ

How do I use a skin I made in Delta?

  1. In the editor, tap Build/Share to export the .deltaskin file.
  2. Save it to Files, then in Delta go to Settings > Controller Skins and import it.
  3. Start a GBA game — the skin applies to that system.

My skin doesn't show up / looks wrong in Delta

  • Check you are playing a GBA game (v1 supports GBA only; other systems are not in yet).
  • Check orientation: portrait and landscape are separate representations — export and import the one matching how you play.
  • Re-export from the editor and re-import; if it still fails, email us the .deltaskin file plus your iOS and Delta versions and we will reproduce it.

Can I import an existing Delta skin and edit it?

No. A .deltaskin is one flat artwork image with the buttons baked in — only the touch rectangles are movable, so moving a button would leave its art behind and break the skin. That is why the editor builds skins from a template + theme instead of editing other people's finished skins.

Can I use my own photo as the background?

Yes — background-image import is free. Buttons and the screen bezel are always drawn by the theme, so the look stays coherent. One photo is used for both orientations (no per-orientation on/off in v1).

What is free and what is paid?

At launch (v1.0), everything is free — no paywall, no ads: fullscreen editing, drag/snap placement, all layouts, unlimited projects, unlimited export, background import, action buttons (fast-forward, quick save/load), and the standard theme. Premium themes are planned for a later update as optional extras; nothing that is free today will ever become paid.

What is the watermark on my export?

Exported skins carry a small credit mark ("Kawapad"), placed automatically where it does not overlap buttons. It is how people who see your skin can find the app.
